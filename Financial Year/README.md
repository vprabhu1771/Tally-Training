A **Financial Year (FY)** is a 12-month period used for accounting, taxation, budgeting, and financial reporting.

### India Financial Year

In India, the financial year runs from:

**1 April to 31 March**

Examples:

| Financial Year | Period                   |
| -------------- | ------------------------ |
| FY 2024–25     | 1 Apr 2024 – 31 Mar 2025 |
| FY 2025–26     | 1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026 |
| FY 2026–27     | 1 Apr 2026 – 31 Mar 2027 |

### Assessment Year (AY)

The Assessment Year is the year immediately following the Financial Year in which income is assessed for tax purposes.

| Financial Year (FY) | Assessment Year (AY) |
| ------------------- | -------------------- |
| FY 2024–25          | AY 2025–26           |
| FY 2025–26          | AY 2026–27           |
| FY 2026–27          | AY 2027–28           |

### Current Financial Year

As of May 2026, the current financial year in India is:

**FY 2026–27 (1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027)**.
